Permission-gated data — you must allow these

The data below requires your explicit browser permission. Click each button to see what a site would receive if you approved. Many apps request these and store them permanently.

📍 Precise GPS Location
IDLE

What sites get: Latitude/longitude accurate to meters, altitude if available, speed, heading, and accuracy radius. Most dangerous of all permissions — combines with your IP location to confirm your real address. Weather apps, maps, and many ad networks request this.

🎙️ Camera & Microphone
IDLE

What sites get: A list of all your camera and microphone device names and IDs — without actually capturing any audio or video. Device names can contain make/model info useful for fingerprinting. Video call sites (Zoom, Meet, etc.) always request this.

🔋 Battery Status
IDLE

What sites get: Battery level (0–100%), charging state, time to charge/discharge. Used in 2015 to track users across sessions — battery level changes slowly and predictably, making it a reliable fingerprint signal. Now restricted in most browsers.

📶 Network / Connection Info
IDLE

What sites get: Connection type (wifi, cellular, ethernet), effective speed (4g/3g/2g/slow-2g), estimated downlink bandwidth, and round-trip time. Used by streaming sites to adapt quality — also a fingerprinting vector.

📋 Clipboard Read
IDLE

What sites get: The full text content of your clipboard — whatever you last copied. Could be passwords, addresses, sensitive messages, crypto keys. Requires explicit permission in modern browsers, but some older sites/extensions can read it silently.
